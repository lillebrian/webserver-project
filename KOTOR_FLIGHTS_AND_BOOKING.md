### **Route Options from Copenhagen (CPH)**

There are **NO direct flights** from Copenhagen to Montenegro. You'll need to connect or fly to a nearby airport.

---

### **OPTION 1: Copenhagen → Tivat (TIV)** ⭐ BEST - Closest to Kotor!

**Distance from Tivat to Kotor:** 8 km (15 minutes)

**Airlines & Typical Routes:**

**1. Via Belgrade (Most Common)**
- **Airline:** Air Serbia
- **Route:** CPH → BEG → TIV
- **Flight time:** 5-7 hours (including layover)
- **Frequency:** Daily in summer
- **Typical price:** €200-400 per person return
- **Book at:** https://www.airserbia.com

**2. Via Vienna**
- **Airline:** Austrian Airlines
- **Route:** CPH → VIE → TIV
- **Flight time:** 5-7 hours
- **Typical price:** €250-450 per person return
- **Book at:** https://www.austrian.com

**3. Via Istanbul**
- **Airline:** Turkish Airlines
- **Route:** CPH → IST → TIV
- **Flight time:** 6-9 hours
- **Typical price:** €300-500 per person return
- **Book at:** https://www.turkishairlines.com

**Search for Tivat flights:**
- **Google Flights:** https://www.google.com/travel/flights?q=flights%20from%20copenhagen%20to%20tivat

**3. Via Munich/Frankfurt/Vienna**

## Airport Transfer to Kotor

### **FROM TIVAT AIRPORT (TIV) - 8 km to Kotor** ⭐

**OPTION 1: Rental Car Pickup** - MOST CONVENIENT
- **Cost:** €0 (included in rental)
- **Time:** 15 minutes
- **Process:** Pick up car at airport, drive directly to Kotor
- **Recommended:** Yes! Best option if renting car

**Car Rental Desks at Tivat Airport:**
- Europcar: https://www.europcar.com
- Sixt: https://www.sixt.com
- Hertz: https://www.hertz.com
- Meridian (local): https://www.meridian-rentacar.com
- Green Motion: https://www.greenmotion.com

---

**OPTION 2: Private Transfer**
- **Cost:** €15-25 for car (up to 4 people)
- **Time:** 15 minutes
- **Process:** Pre-book, driver meets you at arrivals
- **Recommended:** If not renting car immediately

**Book via:**
- **GetTransfer:** https://gettransfer.com/en/
- **Welcome Pickups:** https://www.welcomepickups.com/montenegro/tivat-airport/
- **Kiwi Taxi:** https://kiwitaxi.com/Montenegro/Tivat+airport
- **Local companies:** Just email/WhatsApp, usually cheaper

**Direct Local Companies:**
- **Montenegro Stars:** https://www.montenegrostars.com (transfers & tours)
- **Kotor Airport Transfer:** Email - info@kotorairporttransfer.com
- **Blue Kotor Bay:** https://bluekotorbay.com

---

**OPTION 3: Taxi**
- **Cost:** €10-20 (should be metered)
- **Time:** 15 minutes
- **Process:** Taxi rank outside arrivals
- **Note:** Agree on price before departure or ensure meter is running

**Official Taxi:** Usually white cars with taxi sign
**Estimated fare:** €10-15, but some charge tourists €20

---

**OPTION 4: Bus**
- **Cost:** €2-4 per person
- **Time:** 30-45 minutes
- **Schedule:** Limited, not always convenient for flight times
- **Process:** Bus stop outside airport
- **Recommended:** Only if very budget-conscious and light luggage

**Bus Companies:**
- Check at airport information desk
- Not reliable for specific flight times

---

**RECOMMENDED:** Pick up rental car at Tivat Airport (most convenient)

---

### **FROM DUBROVNIK AIRPORT (DBV) - 95 km to Kotor**

**OPTION 1: Rental Car Pickup**
- **Cost:** €0 (included in rental) + €15-20 fuel
- **Time:** 1.5-2 hours drive
- **Border crossing:** Croatia → Montenegro (usually quick, have passport ready)
- **Process:** Pick up car, drive along coast road
- **Scenic route!** Beautiful drive along Adriatic coast

**Car Rental at Dubrovnik Airport:**

- Oryx (local): https://www.oryx-rent.hr
- **Note:** Check if cross-border to Montenegro is allowed (most allow it)

---

**OPTION 2: Private Transfer**
- **Cost:** €80-120 for car (up to 4 people)
- **Time:** 1.5-2 hours
- **Process:** Pre-book, driver meets you

**Book via:**
- **GetTransfer:** https://gettransfer.com/en/transfer/dubrovnik-airport-dbv-to-kotor

**OPTION 3: Shared Shuttle**
- **Cost:** €25-35 per person
- **Time:** 2-2.5 hours (multiple stops)
- **Process:** Pre-book online

**Companies:**
- **Korkyra:** Email for booking
- **Gateway Montenegro:** Check at airport

---

**OPTION 4: Bus**
- **Cost:** €15-20 per person
- **Time:** 2-3 hours
- **Schedule:** 2-3 daily buses
- **Process:** Bus station in Dubrovnik (need to get from airport to bus station first)
- **Note:** Not convenient with luggage and flight schedules

---

**RECOMMENDED from Dubrovnik:** 
- Rent car if planning to rent anyway (most flexible)
- Private transfer if not renting car (split between 2 = €40-60 each)
