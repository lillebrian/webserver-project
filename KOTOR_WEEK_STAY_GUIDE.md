## Why Kotor is Perfect

**Excellent choice!** Kotor is the ideal base for your Montenegro adventure:

✅ **Perfect Location:** Central to most attractions  
✅ **Beautiful Setting:** Surrounded by mountains and bay  
✅ **Day Trip Hub:** Easy access to beaches, mountains, and historic sites  
✅ **Variety:** Mix of culture, nature, and coastal beauty  
✅ **Walkable:** No car needed within the town  
✅ **Authentic:** Less party-focused than Budva, more genuine atmosphere

**Distances from Kotor:**
- Perast: 12 km (15 min)
- Budva: 23 km (30 min)
- Sveti Stefan: 35 km (45 min)
- Lovćen National Park: 30 km (45 min)
- Cetinje: 40 km (1 hour)
- Durmitor National Park: 170 km (3.5 hours)
- Lake Skadar (Virpazar): 50 km (1 hour)
- Tivat Airport: 8 km (15 min)

## Accommodation Options

### Budget-Friendly Options (for 7 nights for 2 people)

#### **OPTION 1: Apartments in Old Town Kotor** ⭐ Most Atmospheric

**Apartment Types & Pricing:**

**1. Studio Apartment in Old Town**
- **Price:** €350-500/week (€50-70/night)
- **Features:** Historic building, stone walls, compact kitchen, WiFi
- **Pros:** Ultimate location, medieval atmosphere, walking distance to everything
- **Cons:** Steep stairs (no elevators), can be noisy from tourists/bars
- **Best for:** Those who want to be in the heart of the action

**Example Listings:**
- "Old Town Studio Kotor" - ~€400/week
- "Historic Stone Apartment" - ~€450/week
- "Kotor Center Apartment" - ~€380/week

**Where to Book:**
- Booking.com (search "Kotor Old Town apartments")
- Airbnb (filter by "Stari Grad Kotor")
- Direct booking: Many have websites or WhatsApp

---

#### **OPTION 2: Apartments in Dobrota** ⭐ BEST VALUE

**What is Dobrota?**
Quiet coastal village directly adjacent to Kotor (1-2 km), along the bay with beautiful water views.

**Pricing:**
- **Budget Apartments:** €280-400/week (€40-57/night)
- **Sea View Apartments:** €350-500/week (€50-70/night)
- **Luxury Apartments:** €500-700/week (€70-100/night)

**Features:**
- Kitchen facilities (save money cooking)
- Balcony/terrace (often with sea view)
- WiFi, air conditioning
- Parking (if you rent a car)
- Washing machine (useful for week-long stay)

**Pros:**
- Much quieter than Old Town
- Better value for money
- Sea views common
- Parking available
- Larger spaces
- 15-20 min walk to Kotor Old Town

**Cons:**
- Need to walk or drive to Old Town
- Fewer restaurants immediately nearby (but still some good ones)

**Specific Recommendations:**

1. **"Apartments Perovic Dobrota"**
   - €320-380/week
   - Sea view, terrace, kitchen
   - Excellent reviews
   - 1.5 km from Old Town

2. **"Villa Nautica"**
   - €400-500/week
   - Beautiful sea view apartments
   - Modern amenities
   - Swimming area nearby

3. **"Kotor Bay View Apartments"**
   - €350-450/week
   - Balcony with panoramic views
   - Kitchen, WiFi, AC
   - Free parking

**Where to Book:**
- Booking.com (search "Dobrota apartments")
- Airbnb (search "Dobrota, Kotor")
- Direct: Email/WhatsApp (often 10% cheaper)

### **CAR RENTAL** (7 days)

**Why rent a car?**
- Freedom to explore at your own pace
- Access to remote beaches and mountains
- Easier to visit multiple places in a day
- Cost-effective for couples compared to tours/taxis

**Pricing:**

| Car Type | Price Range (7 days) | Fuel Estimate |
|----------|---------------------|---------------|
| Economy (Fiat Panda, VW Up) | €140-210 | €60-80 |
| Compact (VW Golf, Renault Clio) | €175-245 | €70-90 |
| SUV (Dacia Duster) | €245-350 | €90-110 |

**Recommended:** Economy or Compact car

**Total Car Cost (including fuel):** €200-280 for the week

**Additional Costs:**
- **Insurance:** Usually €7-15/day (€50-100/week) - HIGHLY RECOMMENDED
  - Full coverage (CDW + theft) recommended for mountain roads
- **Additional driver:** €3-5/day (€20-35/week) if both want to drive
- **GPS:** €5/day (€35/week) - Skip this, use Google Maps
- **Parking:** Mostly free, €1-2/hour in some areas

**Total with Insurance:** €250-380/week

---

**Car Rental Companies in Kotor/Tivat:**

1. **Meridian Rentacar** (Local, good prices)
   - Economy: €20-25/day
   - Website: meridian-rentacar.com
   - Pickup: Tivat Airport or Kotor

2. **Europcar** (International)
   - Economy: €25-35/day
   - Website: europcar.com
   - Airport pickup

3. **Sixt** (International)
   - Economy: €25-30/day
   - Website: sixt.com
   - Airport pickup

4. **Local Budget Companies:**
   - Green Motion Montenegro
   - Monte Car Rent
   - Usually 20-30% cheaper than international brands

**Tips:**
- Book in advance (2-4 weeks) for best prices
- Pick up at Tivat Airport (often cheaper than city)
- Return with full tank to avoid fees
- Take photos of car condition before leaving
- Check insurance coverage carefully

**Fuel Costs:**
- Current price: ~€1.50-1.60/liter
- Tank fill-up: ~€60-70
- Expected usage for week with day trips: 1-1.5 tanks

---

**ALTERNATIVE: No Car Option**

If you skip the car:
- **Savings:** €250-380
- **Transportation:** Buses, taxis, organized tours
- **Buses from Kotor:** €3-15 per person per trip
- **Estimated weekly transport cost:** €60-100 for two people
- **Net savings:** €150-280

**Recommended:** Rent car for flexibility, but doable without if budget is tight

## Additional Tips for Kotor Stay

### **Best Time of Day:**

**Old Town Kotor:**
- **Early morning (7-9am):** Best for walls climb, no crowds, cooler
- **Late afternoon (6-8pm):** Nice light, fewer tourists
- **Avoid midday:** Cruise ship crowds (10am-4pm)

**Restaurants:**
- Book ahead for waterfront dining (especially Stari Mlini)
- Lunch specials often better value than dinner

### **Parking in Kotor:**

- **Old Town:** Very limited, €2-3/hour
- **Dobrota:** Usually free at apartment
- **Most attractions:** Free or cheap parking

### **Swimming Spots:**

- Along Dobrota waterfront (free, easy access)
- Beach at "Fjord Beach Bar" (free)
- Nearby beaches: Bajova Kula, Morinj

### **Shopping:**

- **Old Town:** Tourist prices, nice souvenirs
- **Kamelija Market:** Local market for produce
- **Supermarkets:** Better prices outside walls

### **WiFi:**

- All accommodation has WiFi
- Most cafes/restaurants have free WiFi
- Consider local SIM if needed (€10 for tourist plan)
