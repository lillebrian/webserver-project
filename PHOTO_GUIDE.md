## 📊 What to Expect Visually

### **Kotor Area:**
- **Architecture:** Medieval stone buildings, marble streets, Venetian influence
- **Colors:** Warm stone (beige/cream), terracotta roofs, blue Adriatic, green mountains
- **Atmosphere:** Historic, romantic, pedestrian-friendly, cats everywhere!
- **Scenery:** Dramatic mountains meeting calm bay waters

### **Beaches:**
- **Type:** Mostly pebble/small stone (not sand)
- **Water:** Crystal clear, blue-green color
- **Setting:** Mountains as backdrop to beaches
- **Facilities:** Beach bars, sunbed rentals, water sports

### **Mountains:**
- **Durmitor:** Alpine-style peaks, glacial lakes, pine forests
- **Lovćen:** Barren limestone peaks, Mediterranean vegetation below
- **Views:** Absolutely spectacular panoramas

### **Food Presentation:**
- **Portions:** GENEROUS (come hungry!)
- **Style:** Rustic, authentic, not fancy presentation
- **Seafood:** Simply grilled, fresh catch displayed
- **Meat:** Grilled platters, traditional serving
