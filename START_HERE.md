## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Is Kotor a good base for a week?**  
A: YES! Perfect location for day trips to beaches, mountains, and historic sites.

**Q: Do we need a car?**  
A: Highly recommended for flexibility. Possible without, but you'll miss some remote areas.

**Q: What's the best airport to fly into from Copenhagen?**  
A: Tivat (closest to Kotor) OR Dubrovnik (often cheaper flights, 1.5hr drive).

**Q: When should we book flights?**  
A: ASAP - prices increase closer to summer. Aim for May 2026 at latest.

**Q: Is €1,800 realistic for both of us?**  
A: Yes! See detailed breakdowns in the guides. Can be done for €1,300 if very budget-conscious.

**Q: Do we need to speak the language?**  
A: No - English widely spoken in tourist areas. Learning a few phrases is appreciated!

**Q: Is Montenegro safe?**  
A: Very safe for tourists. Standard precautions apply.

**Q: Best time to visit in summer?**  
A: Late June or early September = fewer crowds, cheaper, still great weather.  
Late July (your dates) = Peak season but still good!
